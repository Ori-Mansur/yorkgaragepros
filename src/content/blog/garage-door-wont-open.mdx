## ❄️ Special: Extreme Cold Weather Issues

<div className="cold-weather-section">
  <div className="cold-intro">
    <strong>Barrie, Bradford, Innisfil & Northern Simcoe County residents:</strong> -20°C to -30°C creates unique problems.
  </div>

  <div className="cold-issues">
    <div className="cold-issue">
      <h4>🧊 Frozen to Ground</h4>
      <p>Moisture freezes door to concrete. Door won't budge at all.</p>
      <details>
        <summary><strong>How to fix →</strong></summary>
        <ol>
          <li><strong>DON'T force it</strong> - you'll damage the seal or bend door</li>
          <li>Use heat gun/hair dryer on bottom seal (15-20 min)</li>
          <li>Pour warm water along seal</li>
          <li>Gently lift once ice melts</li>
        </ol>
        <p><strong>Prevention:</strong> Keep garage above 5°C. Apply silicone to seal before first snow.</p>
      </details>
    </div>

    <div className="cold-issue">
      <h4>🥶 Stiffened Lubricant</h4>
      <p>Old grease thickens in extreme cold. Door operates sluggishly.</p>
      <details>
        <summary><strong>How to fix →</strong></summary>
        <p><strong>Solution:</strong> Use <u>silicone-based spray</u> (NOT WD-40). Apply to rollers, hinges, springs, tracks.</p>
        <p><strong>Why silicone?</strong> Stays fluid down to -40°C. Essential in Barrie/Bradford/Innisfil.</p>
      </details>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
