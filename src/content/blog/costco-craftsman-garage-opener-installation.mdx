### The Technical Reality: Beyond the Assembly
Assembling the rail is the easy part. The real challenge—and where we see the most "DIY fails"—is in the **wiring and calibration**:

* **Force Calibration:** A 1-1/4 HP motor is incredibly strong. If the travel limits and downward force aren't calibrated to your door's specific weight, the motor can actually damage your door panels or strip the internal nylon gears.
* **Sensor & Camera Logic:** Wiring the safety eyes and syncing the HD camera to home Wi-Fi can be a multi-hour headache. We ensure the signal is clean and the app is fully functional before we leave.
* **The "System" Check:** We don't just hang the motor. We perform a full 10-point inspection to ensure your springs and cables are balanced, so your new opener isn't overworking from day one.
