A wall mount opener, also called a jackshaft, doesn't hang from your ceiling at all. It sits on the wall right beside your door and turns the torsion bar, the long steel bar with the springs above your door, directly. No rail, no chain, no belt running down the middle of your garage.

## What it's made for
Wall mounts are made for garages where the ceiling matters: high ceilings, low ceilings, cathedral ceilings, or a ceiling you'd rather use for storage, lighting or a car lift.

## Why people pick one
- **Your ceiling back.** Nothing hanging over your car. Room for storage racks, a lift, or just a clean look.
- **Very quiet.** The motor sits on the wall, so there's almost no vibration going into the rooms above.
- **Clean and modern.** No long rail across the garage. It's the neatest-looking setup you can get.

## The honest downside
It's the most expensive of the three. It also needs the right setup: your door has to use torsion springs (the ones on a bar above the door), and there needs to be room and power on the wall beside the door. Not every garage qualifies. We'll check before you spend a dollar.

## Who it's right for
Garages with high or low ceilings, anyone planning storage or a car lift, rooms above the garage, and homeowners who want the cleanest, quietest setup available.
