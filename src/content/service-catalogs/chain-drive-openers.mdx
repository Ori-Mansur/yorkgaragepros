A chain drive opener is the classic one. If you grew up with a garage, that's probably the one you heard rattling every morning. A metal chain, like a heavy bike chain, runs along a rail on your ceiling and pulls the door up and down.

## What it's made for
Chain drives are built to do one thing: lift a door, every day, for a long time, without fuss. They handle heavy doors well, including wood and double doors, and they don't care much about cold, heat or dust.

## Why people pick one
- **Price.** It's usually the most affordable way to get a reliable opener.
- **Strength.** Metal on metal. Heavy doors are no problem.
- **Easy to service.** Parts are simple and common, so repairs are quick and cheap.

## The honest downside
They're louder. You'll hear it running, and you'll feel a bit of vibration. If your garage is detached, or there's no bedroom above it, most people never think about it again. If someone sleeps right above the garage, I'd steer you toward a belt drive or a wall mount instead.

## Who it's right for
Detached garages, heavy doors, and anyone who wants solid and simple without paying for quiet.
