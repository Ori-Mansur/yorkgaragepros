A belt drive works just like a chain drive, with one big difference: instead of a metal chain, it uses a reinforced rubber belt to pull your door along the rail. Same job, a lot less noise.

## What it's made for
Belt drives are made for homes where noise matters. If there's a bedroom, a nursery or a home office above or beside your garage, this is usually the one we recommend.

## Why people pick one
- **Quiet.** No metal-on-metal clatter. Most of what you'll hear is the door itself moving.
- **Smooth.** Softer starts and stops, less vibration through the ceiling and walls.
- **Low maintenance.** No chain to grease, and no chain to slap around or stretch.

## The honest downside
It costs a bit more than a chain drive. For most homes the difference is small, but if your garage is detached and nobody's bothered by noise, you're paying for quiet you don't need.

## Who it's right for
Attached garages, rooms above the garage, light sleepers, and anyone who just wants the door to open without waking up the house.
