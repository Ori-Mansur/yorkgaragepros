## Three types, three jobs
Every opener does the same thing: lifts your door. The difference is how, and what that means for noise, space and price.

- **[Chain drive](/garage-door-opener/chain-drive/)**: tough, simple and affordable. A little louder. Great for detached garages and heavy doors.
- **[Belt drive](/garage-door-opener/belt-drive/)**: same idea, but with a reinforced belt instead of a chain. Much quieter. Best when there's a room above the garage.
- **[Wall mount](/garage-door-opener/wall-mount/)**: mounts beside the door instead of on the ceiling. Quietest, cleanest and frees up your ceiling. Needs the right door setup.

## What today's openers can do
Openers have come a long way. Most new models now offer:
- **Wi-Fi and an app.** Open or close from your phone, from anywhere.
- **Alerts.** Get notified when the door opens or closes, or if you left it open.
- **Guest access.** Let family, a dog walker or a delivery driver in without handing out a remote.
- **Battery backup.** The door still works when the power goes out.
- **Built-in LED lighting.** Bright, long-lasting, no bulbs to replace.
- **Quieter DC motors.** Smoother starts and stops, easier on your door.

Some apps charge a monthly fee for extras like video or delivery features. The basics are usually free. We'll tell you which is which before you buy.
